## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges structured academic clarity with the punchy, optimistic energy of modern neo-brutalism and clean comic panels. It creates a focused, friendly, and high-legibility interface tailored for learning, research, and technical productivity without cold corporate austerity or sterile academic fatigue.

Core visual hallmarks:
- **Neo-Brutalist Comic Structure:** Heavy, unapologetic 3px pure black borders (`#000000`) and rigid, un-blurred offset drop shadows ground every panel, card, and interactive control.
- **Airy & Cheerful Atmosphere:** A bright sky-cyan canvas (`#EAF5FC`) and pure white cards (`#FFFFFF`) eliminate muddy or dark backdrops, keeping reading fatigue minimal.
- **Physical Mechanical Feedback:** Clickable surfaces mimic tactile comic cutouts. Interacting with elements causes them to push down and to the right into their own drop shadows with crisp zero-shadow flat contact states.
- **Disciplined Palette Restraint:** Multi-colored distractions (purple, lime, orange, brown) are eliminated. The system relies strictly on deep blues, clean navies, sky washes, and deliberate singular accents (yellow for Beta/highlights, brick red for external/danger).

## Colors

The color system enforces extreme visual clarity and AAA accessibility using a deliberate, clean academic palette free from decorative gradients, glows, or unvetted chromatic noise.

- **Primary Interactive (`#0E4A6E`):** Deep oceanic blue reserved for primary interactive buttons, highlighted focus tracks, and prominent active selections. Always paired with crisp white text.
- **Secondary / Headings (`#0B2E4B`):** Authoritative deep navy engineered for high-impact title blocks, display headers, and foundational structural markers.
- **Tertiary / Panel Canvas (`#D9EDFA`):** Soft sky-tinted structural blue used for secondary containers, sidebar toolboxes, docked bars, and grouped sub-panels.
- **Neutral / Body Copy (`#4E7390`):** Softened yet high-legibility slate-blue for body paragraphs, secondary metadata, and supportive notes, reducing stark visual glare against white paper surfaces.
- **Page Background (`#EAF5FC`):** Bright, cheerful tinted canvas providing continuous atmospheric lift behind pure white card surfaces.
- **Card Background (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure stark white applied to content tiles, interactive sheets, and primary reading surfaces.
- **Ink Black (`#000000`):** Universal 3px keyline borders and crisp offset drop shadows.
- **Accent Yellow (`#FFD02B`):** Strictly restricted to Beta tags, spotlight highlights, and high-priority callout ribbons.
- **Brick Red (`#D93A2B`):** Strictly restricted to destructive prompts, errors, and external outbound link badges.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances expressive comic display titles with fatigue-free academic readability.

- **Headlines & Display (Bricolage Grotesque):** Expressive, punchy, and dense. Headlines deliver the visual punch of vintage graphic comic frames and modern posters. Always set in Navy (`#0B2E4B`).
- **Body & Long-form (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Highly legible, balanced, and humanist. Rendered in Muted Blue (`#4E7390`) to provide soft, high-contrast, non-glaring readability over long sessions.
- **Labels, Codes & Badges (Space Grotesk):** Geometric, monolinear, and technical. Handles metadata tags, beta indicators, counter tallies, and micro-labels. All `label-caps` must use uppercase transformation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px modular cadence structured around an intentional multi-column comic panel system:

- **Mobile Viewports (< 768px):** Single-column stack with `1rem` (16px) margins. Elements span the full safe width to prevent awkward text wrapping within bordered cards.
- **Tablet Viewports (768px - 1023px):** 6-column fluid grid with `1rem` gutters and `2rem` margins, enabling 2-column card layouts and side toolbars.
- **Desktop Viewports (1024px+):** 12-column rigid grid with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` canvas margins (max container width 1280px). Layouts mimic asymmetric comic book panels (e.g., 3-column reference drawers, 6-column core work areas, 3-column metadata sidebars).
- **Rhythm & Padding:** All element gaps and component insets strictly scale using `space-xs` through `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects soft ambient blur shadows, diffuse z-depth gradients, and glassmorphism. Visual depth is established exclusively through pure solid ink keylines and hard offset comic drop shadows.

- **Standard Card & Module Elevation:** Outlined with a continuous `3px solid #000000` border paired with an un-blurred drop shadow: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px 0px #000000`.
- **Raised & Overlay Elevation:** Dialog modals, floating toolbars, and sticky header bars carry an intensified offset: `box-shadow: 6px 6px 0px 0px #000000` with `3px solid #000000` border.
- **Interactive Stamp Shift:** Hover states maintain the 4px shadow or subtly expand to 5px. Active/pressed states execute a mechanical physical press: `transform: translate(4px, 4px)` with `box-shadow: 0px 0px 0px 0px #000000`. The element physically snaps flush with the canvas.
- **Flat Inset Surfaces:** Embedded sub-panels (`#D9EDFA`) placed inside white cards do not use drop shadows; they are partitioned cleanly by a `3px solid #000000` border.

## Shapes

The geometry strikes a deliberate contrast between structured rectangular panels and softened, friendly edges:

- **Cards & Enclosures:** Employ an outer corner radius of `16px` (`rounded-lg` level 2) combined with heavy 3px black strokes. This keeps neo-brutalist forms approachable and comic-like rather than overly sharp or industrial.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Use pill-shaped contours (`9999px` full round) or `12px` roundedness to create strong click affordance against boxy panels.
- **Controls & Checkboxes:** Micro-elements (checkboxes, inner segment tabs) maintain a `6px` to `8px` corner radius to mirror the container's softened profile.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Buttons:** Deep Blue (`#0E4A6E`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Rimmed with a `3px solid #000000` border and `4px 4px 0px #000000` drop shadow. Font: Space Grotesk Bold, 14px. On click, translates `translate(4px, 4px)` with `0px 0px 0px` shadow.
- **Secondary Buttons:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) fill with `#0B2E4B` text, `3px solid #000000` border, and `4px 4px 0px #000000` shadow.
- **Destructive Buttons:** Brick Red (`#D93A2B`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text, `3px solid #000000` border, and `4px 4px 0px #000000` shadow.

### Cards & Panels
- **Standard Cards:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `3px solid #000000`, shadow `4px 4px 0px #000000`, border-radius `16px`, padding `1.5rem`.
- **Sub-Panels:** Background `#D9EDFA`, border `3px solid #000000`, border-radius `12px`, padding `1rem`. Used for auxiliary notes, code blocks, or nested tool panels.

### Badges & Chips
- **Beta Badge:** Fill `#FFD02B`, text `#000000`, border `2px solid #000000`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #000000`, radius `9999px`, font `label-caps`. Strictly reserved for Beta indicators and primary spotlight flags.
- **External Badge / Danger Chip:** Fill `#D93A2B`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `2px solid #000000`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #000000`, radius `9999px`, font `label-caps`. Strictly reserved for outbound link notices and critical warning tags.
- **Standard Filter Chip:** Fill `#FFFFFF`, text `#0B2E4B`, border `2px solid #000000`, shadow `2px 2px 0px #000000`. Active state fills `#0E4A6E` with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Input Fields
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`, border `3px solid #000000`, shadow `3px 3px 0px #000000`, border-radius `10px`, height `48px`, padding `0 1rem`.
- Text: `#0B2E4B`, placeholder text `#4E7390`.
- Focus state: Outline remains `3px solid #000000`, shadow sharpens to `5px 5px 0px #000000`, background shifts to `#EAF5FC`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- 22x22px square (checkbox, 6px radius) or circle (radio). Border: `3px solid #000000`, shadow: `2px 2px 0px #000000`.
- Selected state: Fill `#0E4A6E` with sharp white tick or center dot.

### Lists & Dividers
- List items feature solid `#000000` 2px dividers between rows or exist as independently separated micro-cards with `2px 2px 0px #000000` shadows.
